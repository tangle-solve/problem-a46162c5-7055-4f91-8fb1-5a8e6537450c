### CHEM-000005: Position-programmable carbon-to-nitrogen ring editing
- **Subfield:** Organic synthesis and medicinal chemistry
- **Open:** Replace a selected ring carbon with nitrogen in densely substituted arenes or heteroarenes while preserving the remaining substitution pattern. Independently choose between competing replacement positions using practical reagents.
- **Status:** Rapidly developing since the early 2020s. Pyridine-to-pyridazine conversion was demonstrated in 2025 through azide introduction and photochemical rearrangement; general positional editing remains open.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Makes medicinal scaffold changes possible late in a synthesis.
- **Key works:** Puriņš, Nakahara and Levin (2025), *Bridging the pyridine-pyridazine synthesis gap by skeletal editing*, [DOI:10.1126/science.adx4762](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adx4762).
- **Related:** Computer science: constraint-preserving graph rewriting for synthesis planning.
